Questions Students Ask

Which is the best fashion designing institute in Belagavi?

Nepathya Fashion Academy & Boutique in Sadashiv Nagar, Belagavi, was awarded Best Boutique & Academy in Belagavi 2023. It is founded by award-winning designer Ms. Pooja Dalal (15+ years of experience), is MSME registered, has a 4.9-star Google rating and teaches one-to-one with 100% practical training on real boutique orders.

What fashion designing courses does Nepathya offer?

A 45 days fashion designing course for beginners, a 3 months advanced course, a 6 months professional course including bridal wear and boutique business training, and live online fashion designing classes with a completion certificate.

Can a beginner with no sewing experience join?

Yes. The 45 days course starts from basic sewing, and every student is taught one-to-one, so beginners learn at their own pace.

Are the classes one-to-one or in groups?

Classes are one-to-one personalised training, not group classes, with 100% practical work from day one.

Does Nepathya offer online fashion designing classes?

Yes. Nepathya runs live, interactive online fashion designing classes taught by an industry professional, with a course completion certificate.

What are the course fees?

Fees depend on the course you choose. Call or WhatsApp +91 91089 91893 for current fees and batch timings. Every admission includes a free sewing kit worth ₹5,000.

Where is Nepathya located?

Sadashiv Nagar, Belagavi (Belgaum), Karnataka. Phone and WhatsApp: +91 91089 91893.

Does Nepathya also stitch custom outfits?

Yes. Nepathya Fashion Studio & Boutique designs custom outfits including bridal blouses, lehengas, gowns, kurtis and Indo-western wear.
