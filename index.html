Welcome To Nepathya

Nepathya is a fashion design studio for people who want clothes made for them, not pulled off a rack. We sketch every piece around your occasion, your shape and your taste, then cut, stitch and embroider it by hand.

From a bridal lehenga to a reception gown or a single statement blouse, you work directly with the designer from first sketch to final fitting.
